# Think Deeper

When asked to solve a complex problem, do not jump straight to implementation.

Follow this protocol:

1. Restate the problem in your own words.
2. Identify constraints: what must happen and what must not happen.
3. Explore two or three approaches with tradeoffs.
4. Ask clarifying questions if requirements are ambiguous.
5. Recommend the best approach with justification.
6. Implement only after the direction is clear.

Avoid premature optimization, over-engineering, unrelated changes, and assuming context you do not have.

Prefer simplicity, explicit behavior, and small changes with clear impact.
